RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
(Sample document for Portia demos. Fictional property, landlord and tenant.)

Property: Unit 4B, Maple Court Residences, 1200 Example Avenue, Springfield
Landlord: Maple Court Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord")
Tenant: Jordan Sample ("Tenant")
Term: 12 months beginning March 1, 2027 and ending February 29, 2028.

1. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,850.00, due on the 1st day of each month, payable through the Resident Portal.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $4,625.00 (two and one-half months of rent). The deposit may be applied to any amounts owed under this Lease.

3. LATE CHARGES. Rent not received by 11:59 p.m. on the 1st day of the month is late. Tenant shall pay an initial late charge of $150.00, plus an additional $20.00 for each day thereafter until rent is paid in full, up to a maximum of $450.00 per month. Partial payments do not stop late charges.

4. REQUIRED MONTHLY SERVICES. The following services are mandatory for all residents, are billed with rent, and are not optional:
   (a) Technology Package (bulk internet and smart-lock access): $45.00 per month.
   (b) Community Amenity Fee: $35.00 per month.
   (c) Valet Trash Service: $25.00 per month.
   (d) Utility Billing Administration Fee: $6.95 per month.

5. MOVE-IN CHARGES. Tenant shall pay a non-refundable Administrative Processing Fee of $250.00 and a non-refundable Lease Preparation Fee of $95.00 at signing.

6. RENEWAL. This Lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms at the then-current market rent unless Tenant delivers written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

7. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant ends this Lease before the end of the term, Tenant shall pay an early termination fee equal to three (3) months of rent, in addition to rent owed through the date the Landlord recovers possession.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant accepts the Premises "as is" and is responsible for all repairs to the Premises and appliances, regardless of cause, except structural repairs to the roof and foundation.

9. GUESTS. No guest may stay more than three (3) nights in any calendar month without the prior written consent of Landlord. Unauthorized occupants are subject to a fee of $25.00 per day.

10. PETS. No pets without a signed pet addendum. Pet rent, if approved, is $50.00 per month per pet.

11. LIABILITY. Tenant waives any claim against Landlord for damage to Tenant's property from any cause, including Landlord's negligence. Tenant shall pay Landlord's attorney fees in any dispute.

12. RENTERS INSURANCE. Tenant must maintain renters insurance with liability coverage of at least $100,000 and name Landlord as an additional insured.

Signed:
____________________ Landlord        ____________________ Tenant
